SECTION  1:  GENERAL  INFORMATION  ABOUT  UCU  
 
1.1  Overview  
Uganda  Christian  University  (UCU)  is  a  private,  chartered,  Christian  university  administered  by  the  Church  of  
Uganda
 
(Anglican).
 
It
 
is
 
one
 
of
 
Uganda's
 
leading
 
teaching
 
and
 
research
 
universities
 
and
 
was
 
the
 
first
 
private
 
university
 
in
 
Uganda
 
to
 
receive
 
a
 
government
 
charter.
 
1.2  Key  Facts  at  a  Glance  
Founded  1997  (as  UCU);  predecessor  Bishop  Tucker  Theological  College,  1913  
Location  (Main  Campus)  Mukono  Hill,  Bishop  Tucker  Road,  P.O.  Box  4,  Mukono,  Uganda  
Distance  from  Kampala  ~25  km  east,  on  the  Kampala–Jinja  Highway  (approx.  1–1.5  hrs  drive)  
Ownership  Church  of  Uganda  (Anglican  Communion)  
Accreditation  National  Council  for  Higher  Education  (NCHE),  Uganda;  Government  Charter  granted  2004  
Chancellor  Most  Rev.  Dr.  Stephen  Samuel  Kaziimba  Mugalu  (Archbishop  of  Uganda,  since  March  2020)  
Vice  Chancellor  Assoc.  Prof.  Aaron  Mushengyezi  (3rd  VC;  holds  PhD  from  University  of  Connecticut)  
Campuses  6  locations  across  Uganda  (Mukono,  Kampala,  Mbale,  Arua,  Kabale,  Kagando)  
Enrolment  Approx.  15,000  students  (Mukono  main  campus);  total  across  all  campuses  ~7,000–15,000+  
Programs  70+  diploma,  bachelor's,  master's,  and  PhD  programmes  
Intakes  per  Year  Three:  Easter  (January),  Trinity  (May),  Advent  (September)  
Medium  of  Instruction  English

3.4  Academic  Calendar  
UCU  operates  on  a  trimester  (three-semester)  system:  
Semester Period Notes 
Semester  1  (Easter)  January  –  April  Largest  intake;  most  programmes  available  
Semester  2  (Trinity)  May  –  August  Mid-year  intake;  some  programmes  only  
Semester  3  (Advent)  September  –  December  Second  major  intake;  all  major  programmes  
Each  semester  lasts  approximately  12–14  weeks.  UCU  holds  7  graduation  ceremonies  annually  across  its

SECTION  5:  ADMISSIONS  
 
5.1  Entry  Requirements  —  Undergraduate  
For  Ugandan  Applicants:  Direct  Entry  (School  Leavers):  •  Uganda  Certificate  of  Education  (UCE)  —  minimum  5  passes  •  Uganda  Advanced  Certificate  of  Education  (UACE)  —  minimum  2  principal  passes  in  relevant  subjects  Alternative  Entry:  •  A  Higher  Education  Certificate  (HEC)  in  a  relevant  field  •  A  Diploma  in  a  relevant  discipline  from  a  recognised  institution  of  Higher  Learning  •  A  pass  in  the  Mature  Age  Entrance  Examination  (for  mature  applicants  who  do  not  have  A-level  
qualifications)
 
For  Kenyan  Applicants:  •  KCSE  holders:  minimum  grade  C+  and  above  •  KACE/'GCE/A-levels':  at  least  2  principal  passes  or  equivalent  •  Diploma  in  a  relevant  discipline  from  a  recognised  institution  
For  Tanzanian  Applicants:  •  TCE  &  TACE  Certificate  with  at  least  2  principal  passes,  OR  •  Diploma  from  a  recognised  institution  of  Higher  Learning  
For  International  Applicants  (other  countries):  •  Qualifications  recognised  for  University  entry  in  the  applicant's  home  country  •  Non-English  transcripts  must  be  officially  translated  and  certified  •  Application  fee:  USD  50  
Special  Notes  on  Competitive  Programmes:  •  Bachelor  of  Laws  (LLB):  Pre-entry  exam  required  •  Bachelor  of  Medicine  and  Surgery  (MBChB):  Pre-entry  exam  required  •  Bachelor  of  Dental  Surgery  (BDS):  Pre-entry  exam  required  •  Master  of  Business  Administration  (MBA):  Pre-entry  exam  required  •  Application  fee  for  pre-entry  exam  programmes:  UGX  150,000  
5.2  Postgraduate  Entry  Requirements  •  A  good  first  degree  from  a  recognised  institution  of  higher  learning,  relevant  to  the  course  applied  
for
 •  Applicants  with  a  Third  Class  degree  may  be  considered  for  some  programmes  depending  on  work

SECTION  12:  POLICIES  &  REGULATIONS  
 
12.1  Grading  System  
UCU  uses  a  percentage-based  grading  system  aligned  with  Uganda's  national  standards:  
Grade Percentage  Range Classification 
A  /  First  Class  80%  and  above  First  Class  Honours  /  Distinction  
B  /  Upper  Second  70%  –  79%  Upper  Second  Class  Honours  (2:1)  
C  /  Lower  Second  60%  –  69%  Lower  Second  Class  Honours  (2:2)  
D  /  Pass  50%  –  59%  Pass  /  Third  Class  
E  /  Fail  Below  50%  Fail  —  retake  required  
NOTE:  Exact  grading  boundaries  may  vary  by  school/programme.  Confirm  with  your  school's  Academic  Handbook. 
12.2  Attendance  Policy  •  Students  must  attend  a  minimum  of  75%  of  lectures  per  course  to  be  eligible  to  sit  examinations  •  Roll  calls  are  conducted  during  lectures  for  attendance  verification  •  Missing  more  than  25%  of  lectures  without  valid  excuse  disqualifies  a  student  from  examinations  for  
that
 
course
 •  This  policy  is  strictly  enforced  —  lecturers  may  have  attendance  verification  officers
